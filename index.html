Quick guide

Lunch with the E-gineering crew

Use the wheel

  • Spin to pick a place. The result stays on screen so you can check its notes, website, menu, and hours.
  • Select Back to wheel when you’re ready to spin again.
  • Tap category filters to include or exclude those types of food. Use the search box to find a place in the list.
  • Use the X beside a place to remove it for now. Restore brings removed places back.
  • Use Add Spot to add a restaurant or Shuffle to change the order.

How our group of four picks

  1. Post the starting wheel link in your lunch Slack thread.
  2. One at a time, each of the four people opens the most recent wheel link in the thread and removes any places they would rather not have. After making changes, choose Share Wheel to post the updated link in the same thread.
  3. The next person always starts from the newest link in the thread, so earlier removals stay out. When all four have had a turn and everyone's ready, open the final link and spin once as a group.

The wheel doesn't sync live. To get one shared pick, use one screen and spin together; spins on separate devices are independent and may pick different places. If Slack cuts off the long link, copy and send the complete URL.

Use the reset button in the header to restore the original default list.
